🗣️ IELTS Speaking — 57 Mock Sets

Each set runs like the real test: an identity check, one Part 1 topic, one Part 2 cue card, and a Part 3 discussion. There are 57 sets in all — one for every Part 2/3 card. The Part 1 topics rotate through #1–#39 and start over from #1 once they run out. The numbering matches your original file.

SET 1Part1 #1 · Part2/3 #1
Introduction — Identity Check
  • Good morning/afternoon. My name’s ______. Could you tell me your full name, please?
  • What can I call you?
  • Could you tell me where you’re from?
  • Could I see your identification, please?
Part 1 — Study
  • Do you work or are you a student?
  • What subjects are you studying?
  • Do you like your subject?
  • Why did you choose to study that subject?
  • Do you think that your subject is popular in your country?
  • Do you have any plans for your studies in the next five years?
  • Do you want to change your major?
  • Do you prefer to study in the mornings or in the afternoons?
  • How much time do you spend on your studies each week?
  • Are you looking forward to working?
  • What technology do you use when you study?
  • What changes would you like to see in your school?
Part 2 — Cue Card
Describe a time when you got up early
You should say:
  • When it was
  • What you did
  • Why you got up early
  • And how you felt about it
Part 3 — Discussion
  • Do you know anyone who likes to get up early?
  • Why do people get up early?
  • What kinds of occasions need people to arrive early?
  • Why do some people like to stay up late?
  • Is it good to arrive early in any situation?
