SET 1Part1: Study · Part2/3 #1
Introduction — Identity Check
  • Good morning/afternoon. My name’s ______. Could you tell me your full name, please?
  • What can I call you?
  • Could you tell me where you’re from?
  • Could I see your identification, please?
Part 1 — Study
  • Do you study or work?
  • What is your major?
  • What are your plans after your studies?
  • Do you want to change your major?
  • Do you prefer to study in the mornings or the afternoons?
  • Are you looking forward to working?
  • How much time do you spend on your studies each week?
  • What changes would you like to see in your school?
Part 2 — Cue Card
Describe a time when you changed your mind about an important decision.
You should say:
  • When you changed the decision
  • What the original decision was
  • Why you changed it
  • And explain how you felt about the experience.
Part 3 — Discussion
  • When do most children begin to make their own decisions?
  • What kinds of decisions should parents allow their children to make?
  • Why do some people find it difficult to change an important decision they have already made?
  • Do you think people make better decisions when they ask other people for advice? Why or why not?
  • What are some important decisions that young people have to make as they become adults?

  • Do you like traveling?

  • Did you have a long journey with your family as a child?
  • Do you take photos of the scenery outside the car window?
  • Do you prefer the mountains or the sea?
Part 2 — Cue Card
Describe a tall building you like or dislike.
You should say:
  • What it is used for
  • Where it is
  • What it looks like
  • And explain why you like/dislike it.
Part 3 — Discussion
  • Are there many tall buildings in your country?
  • What are the differences between those tall buildings in your country?
  • Why are different places laid out and designed differently?
  • What are the advantages of living in tall buildings?
